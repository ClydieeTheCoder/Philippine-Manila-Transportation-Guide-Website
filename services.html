Transport Guide

Every Manila ride, explained

This guide is built around Manila's city buses, LRT 1 & 2, tricycles, and ride-hailing apps — with real named routes, real fares, and a 50% discount for students, senior citizens, and PWDs on every calculator below. Routes outside Manila city limits are not covered.

Bus, LRT, tricycle, or ride-hailing?

🚌 City Bus
₱18 – ₱20
  • Fixed fares between named Manila landmarks — Divisoria, Quiapo, España, Intramuros, and more.
  • Pick your route in the Bus Ticket Machine below for an exact fare, no guesswork.
  • Board at any recognized stop along the route — no fixed terminals needed.
  • Students, senior citizens, and PWDs ride at 50% off with valid ID.
Fixed fare50% discount
🚈 LRT 1 & 2
₱15 – ₱50
  • Fastest way across Manila, skipping street traffic entirely.
  • Fare depends on how many stations apart your origin and destination are.
  • LRT 1 runs Manila's Taft Avenue corridor; LRT 2 runs the Recto–Santolan corridor.
  • Trains run frequently during peak hours, so waits rarely top 5 minutes.
Skip traffic50% discount
🚙 Tricycle
₱40 – ₱60
  • Best for short hops within a neighborhood — a few blocks to about a kilometer.
  • Fare depends on distance and how many passengers are sharing the ride.
  • Look for tricycle terminals near markets, schools, and residential streets.
  • Students, senior citizens, and PWDs ride at 50% off with valid ID.
Short trips50% discount
🚗 Ride-Hailing
₱45 – ₱999
  • Grab, Uber, Angkas, MoveIt, InDrive, and GSM Blue all cover Manila.
  • Pin your pickup and drop-off in the estimator below for a distance-based price.
  • Good for door-to-door trips or when it's raining and the queues are long.
  • Fares rise during rain and rush hour — check more than one app before booking.
Door-to-door50% discount
